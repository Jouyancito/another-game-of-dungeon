## A. Biome Constraint (read before every placement decision)

Floor 1 is an **indoor crystal cavern with a prairie floor**, ~600×600 m, ceiling height 45 m.

| Parameter | Value | Implication |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| **Light source** | Crystal ceiling — filtered, diffuse; 3 SpotLight monarchs + ~12 OmniLights | No direct solar UV. Equivalent to dense forest understory or deep overcast. PAR (photosynthetically active radiation) is low-to-medium, warm-tinted |
| **Ambient fill** | WorldEnvironment ambient_light_energy ≈ 0.13 cool tint | Background is dim, not black — twilight zone level |
| **Humidity** | Cave = high humidity year-round, no wind, stable temperature | Favors ferns, mosses, fungi, moisture-loving shrubs. Discourages xeric / drought-adapted plants |
| **Water POI** | `_build_pond()` — one or more ponds generated by POISystem | Aquatic / hygrophilous species belong ONLY in the pond annular zone (inner_r → outer_r+6m in `_scatter_cluster`) |
| **Soil** | Cave floor, organic debris from crystal drip, bat guano analogue | Nutrient-rich, mesic to hydric. Not rocky scree |
| **Full-sun species** | INCOHERENT — require 6+ h direct solar UV, impossible in cavern | Must be replaced or recontextualized as cave-adapted variants |

**Rule of thumb applied in this sheet**: if a plant in the real world requires ≥4 h/day of direct sun
to bloom or survive, it is flagged INCOHERENT for this biome. Crystal glow ≠ sunlight.

## C. COHERENCE BREAKS — Fix Queue

Prioritized by visual impact on the demo video. Code locations included.

---

### BREAK #1 — CRITICAL: Wildflower clumps scattered throughout entire map (full-sun species in a dim cave)

**Asset**: `env_flower_clump_01/02/03.gltf`
**Problem**: Open meadow wildflowers require 6-8 h/day of direct solar UV. A cave with crystal spotlights cannot substitute. These render as bright daisy/buttercup patches across the cave floor — the single strongest signal that the map is "an outdoor field, not a cavern."
**Impact on video**: Immediately breaks the cavern identity in any wide shot.
**Fix**: Restrict to monarch crystal spotlight zones only. Change scatter so `POOL_GROUND` drops flower clumps only when `pos` is within a configurable radius of a monarch crystal position. OR replace all three with additional `env_mushroom_common_01.gltf` weight in the pool.
**Code location**: `_generate_vegetation()` → `_scatter_pool(POOL_GROUND, ...)` line ~1519 and `_scatter_cluster(POOL_GROUND, ...)` calls for pond/entrance/altar/well POIs (~1487, 1502, 1511).
**Effort**: Low — change pool weights or add a zone-gate condition to `_place_instance`.

---

### BREAK #2 — HIGH: Laetiporus bracket fungus scattered on bare ground (wrong substrate)

**Asset**: `env_mushroom_laetiporus_01.gltf`
**Problem**: Laetiporus sulphureus (Chicken-of-the-woods) is a **wood-decaying bracket fungus**. It grows on the sides and base of dead or dying hardwood trees, never from bare soil. Placing it in `POOL_GROUND` scatters it across open field — ecologically wrong and visually reads as "floating mushroom pancake on grass."
**Impact on video**: Noticeable in close shots. The model's bracket shape makes the floating-on-ground look immediately odd.
**Fix**: Remove from `POOL_GROUND`. Instead, attach it as a child decoration to every instance of `env_tree_dead_01.gltf` during `_place_instance`. A simple approach: after instantiating a dead tree, 40% chance to also instantiate `env_mushroom_laetiporus_01.gltf` at its base, scaled down, with a randomized rotation.
**Code location**: `POOL_GROUND` const at line ~1238-1245. `_place_instance()` at line ~1553. Add a special-case dead-tree placement in `_generate_vegetation()`.
**Effort**: Medium — requires a small placement specialization for dead trees.

---

### BREAK #3 — HIGH: Manicured hedges (prop_hedge_01, prop_hedge_curved_01) placed in cavern

**Asset**: `prop_hedge_01.glb`, `prop_hedge_curved_01.glb`
**Problem**: Trimmed decorative hedges require full sun, human cultivation, and regular trimming. None of these conditions exist in a sealed cave in the "Descubrimiento" phase (no civilization, no gardeners). They signal "English garden" not "crystal cavern." Their geometric trimmed shape is visually incongruous next to organic cave rock.
**Impact on video**: Any shot with a perfectly-trimmed hedge in front of cave crystal destroys the biome read.
**Fix**: Remove from any procedural scatter. If they are in the `.tscn` as curated props, replace with `env_bush_large_01.gltf` + moss material override, or `prop_rock_wide_01.glb` + climbing-vine-style texture.
**Code location**: Check `floor1_prairie.tscn` for any static instances. If in `POOL_BUSHES`, remove. Grep: `prop_hedge` in `.tscn`.
**Effort**: Low — remove/replace references.

---

### BREAK #4 — MEDIUM: Birch trees dominating the canopy (high-light pioneer species in deep cavern shade)

**Asset**: `env_tree_birch_01..05.gltf` — 5 variants, largest share of `POOL_TREES`
**Problem**: Silver/paper birch explicitly requires high light and is described as "cannot grow in the shade." In a dim crystal cavern, a birch forest is incoherent: the trees would be etiolated (abnormally tall and pale), with sparse foliage — which could be artistically intended, but is not the current dense-canopy green rendering.
**Impact on video**: The forest reads as "outdoor birch grove" not "pale cave forest." Contrast with the more coherent maple.
**Fix A (preferred)**: Rebalance `POOL_TREES` weight — reduce birch to 2 variants (down from 5), increase maple to 5 slots and add cave-appropriate deciduous variants. Current pool has 12 entries: 5 birch / 3 maple / 3 common / 1 dead. Target: 2 birch / 5 maple / 3 common / 2 dead.
**Fix B (visual only)**: Change birch material to pale/etiolated colors (near-white canopy, bone-white bark) + reduced scale — communicates "struggling for light" which is ecologically honest and visually interesting.
**Code location**: `POOL_TREES` const at line ~1212-1225. Change array composition.
**Effort**: Low — edit the POOL_TREES array entries.

---

### BREAK #5 — MEDIUM: CSG border walls and POI structures use flat plain-color materials (no cave texture)

**Asset**: CSG border walls, boss arena walls, ruins, pillars — all use `_make_material(COLOR_*)` which produces flat `StandardMaterial3D` with solid albedo color and no normal/roughness map.
**Problem**: Cave stone is never uniformly colored — it has variation, veining, moisture staining. The current flat gray/beige walls read as "placeholder geometry." On camera they look like blockout-phase walls, not carved stone.
**Impact on video**: Every wide shot with a visible border wall or boss arena immediately signals "early alpha geometry." This is the single biggest production-value gap.
**Fix**: Replace `_make_material()` for structural surfaces with a version that adds `roughness = 0.85`, a simple triplanar noise albedo variation (procedural — no texture files needed), and optionally a tiny normal-map bumpiness via ShaderMaterial. For the border walls specifically, adding a CAVE_STONE color variation (dark + light zones, moisture streaks) elevates the entire perimeter.
**Code location**: `_make_material()` at line ~1866. `_build_organic_border()` line ~589. `_build_boss_arena()` line ~993. `_build_ruins()` line ~957.
**Effort**: Medium — replace `_make_material` with `_make_cave_material` that adds roughness + variation.

## E. Quick-Reference — What the Cavern WANTS

Assets that are ecologically **ideal** for this biome (prioritize these for future additions):

| Category | Priority additions |
|----------|--------------------|
| **Fungi** | More common mushroom varieties, shelf fungi on dead wood, bioluminescent cap mushrooms |
| **Ground cover** | Mosses, liverworts, wood sorrel (*Oxalis*), hart's tongue fern, cave violet (*Viola canina*) |
| **Trees** | Pale/etiolated deciduous (white-bark variants), dead snags, hollow logs, root masses |
| **Shrubs** | Unfowering woody shrubs, ivy/vine types, dark-leafed low shrubs |
| **Terrain** | Wet stone, damp soil patches, water seep traces, cave crystal floor fragments |
| **Water zone** | Cattails / reeds (near pond), water-edge mosses, rounded water-worn stones |
| **Man-made** | Torches, rough-hewn stone props, rope, decayed wooden structures (NOT garden furniture) |

Assets that **do not belong** without explicit zone-gating or artistic justification:

- Open meadow wildflowers (flower_clump_*) outside crystal spotlight zones
- Manicured/trimmed hedges
- Bracket fungi on bare ground
- Chimneys isolated from camp structures
- Formal garden props (fountain, hedge) without ruin/decay override
